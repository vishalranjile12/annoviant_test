Platform overview

TxGuard® is Annoviant’s investigational regenerative tissue platform. Biological tissue is decellularized and processed through a proprietary stabilization and sterilization approach to create an off-the-shelf extracellular-matrix scaffold. The platform is designed for host-cell integration and remodeling while addressing calcification, thrombosis, and durability limitations associated with conventional replacement materials.

Proposed mechanism of action

  1. Implantation: an acellular extracellular-matrix scaffold is placed using established surgical technique.
  2. Host integration: endogenous cells migrate into the preserved matrix and repopulate the scaffold.
  3. Remodeling: in preclinical animal models, the scaffold progressively remodels toward host-derived living tissue.

Important notice

TxGuard® products are investigational and have not been approved or cleared for commercial use. The Pulmonary Valved Graft holds FDA Humanitarian Use Device designation (2020); this designation is not FDA approval. Information in this summary is educational, may be updated as development progresses, and should not be used to make treatment decisions.
